# Privacy Policy for Lucky 13 Tiles

**Effective date:** September 23, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 23, 2026

Lucky 13 Tiles (the **“App”**) respects your privacy. The App is designed to be played without creating an account or providing personal information.

## Information We Collect

The developer of Lucky 13 Tiles does not collect, store, transmit, sell, rent, or share your personal data.

The App does not use:

- Advertising or advertising identifiers
- Third-party analytics
- Tracking technologies
- Profiling
- A developer-operated account system
- A developer-operated server or database containing player information

## Information Stored on Your Device

The App may save game information such as settings, puzzle progress, completed puzzles, attempts, times, and streaks locally on your device so that the game can function properly.

This information remains on your device, is not transmitted to or accessible by the developer, and may be removed if you delete the App or clear its data.

## Game Center and Apple Services

Lucky 13 Tiles may offer optional features provided by Apple, such as Game Center leaderboards. If you use these features, Apple may process information associated with your Apple Account or Game Center profile, including your nickname, avatar, scores, achievements, and related activity.

The developer does not operate Game Center and does not store this information on developer-controlled servers. Apple’s collection and use of information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policies. You can learn more at:

<https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/>

## Data Sharing and Sale

Because the developer does not collect personal data, no personal data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

## Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. If you believe personal information has been provided to the developer, please contact us using the information below.

## Data Security

The developer does not maintain a server-side database of player information. Information saved locally by the App is protected by the security features of your device. Information handled by Apple services is protected according to Apple’s own policies and practices.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App’s features or privacy practices change. Any updated version will include a revised “Last updated” date and will be made available wherever this policy is published.
