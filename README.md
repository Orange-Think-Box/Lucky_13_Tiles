# Lucky T1L3S

**Lucky T1L3S** is a compact number-matching puzzle game built around one simple challenge: select available tiles that add up to **13**, remove them from the board, and keep uncovering new possibilities until every tile is gone.

The rules are easy to understand, but clearing a board takes more than quick arithmetic. Every combination changes which tiles become available next. A move that looks perfect now may block a better combination later, so players must balance immediate opportunities with the shape of the board underneath.

## How to Play

Each puzzle begins with a patterned board made from numbered square tiles. Some tiles are immediately available, while others remain covered or blocked by surrounding tiles.

Choose **two to four available tiles** whose values total exactly **13**. A correct combination is removed from the board, exposing new adjacent tiles and creating fresh combinations. Continue finding groups that total 13 until the board is completely cleared.

For example:

- 5 + 8 = 13
- 1 + 4 + 8 = 13
- 1 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 13

Only currently available tiles can be selected. Because removing one group changes access to the tiles beneath or beside it, the order of play matters. The best solution is not always the most obvious one.

## The Goal

Clear every tile from the board.

A successful puzzle requires players to:

- Recognize combinations quickly.
- Think ahead before removing a group.
- Avoid leaving behind tiles that cannot form 13.
- Adapt as new numbers are revealed.
- Complete the board with as few mistakes and attempts as possible.

## Board Patterns

Lucky T1L3S uses different board shapes to keep each puzzle visually distinct and strategically interesting. Current patterns include:

- Stepped Pyramid
- Diamond
- Cross
- Hollow Square

Different shapes can contain different numbers of tiles, starting arrangements, and exposed positions. This means the same group of numbers may play very differently depending on the board pattern.

## Daily Puzzles

The game is designed around a daily challenge. Each day brings a new board to master, giving players a quick puzzle they can enjoy without committing a large part of their day.

Players can track their performance through:

- Completion time
- Number of attempts
- Incorrect combinations
- Undos used
- Tiles remaining
- Daily streak

Previous daily puzzles can also be revisited, allowing missed boards to be played or completed puzzles to be mastered again.

## Attempts and Mastery

Restarting a puzzle resets the board and begins a new attempt. The goal is not simply to stumble through a solution, but to learn the board and improve with each try.

Once the puzzle has been cleared, the completion screen summarizes the result and gives the player the option to play again, return to the main menu, or share the victory.

## Why It Works

Lucky T1L3S combines familiar arithmetic with a layered board-clearing mechanic. The math is approachable, but the puzzle comes from deciding which combination to remove and when.

The result is a game that is:

- Easy to learn in seconds.
- Quick to play in short sessions.
- Strategic enough to reward planning.
- Different each day.
- Satisfying to replay and improve.

The central question is always simple:

**Can you make 13 and clear the board?**
